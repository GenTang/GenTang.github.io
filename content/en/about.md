# About

Gen Tang is a data scientist specializing in artificial intelligence and big data. He is the author of *Mastering Data Science: From Linear Regression to Deep Learning* and *Deconstructing Large Language Models: From Linear Regression to General Intelligence*, and has served as a technical reviewer for the British technology publisher Packt.

He earned dual bachelor's degrees in Mathematics and Computer Science from Fudan University, followed by dual master's degrees in Economics and Data Science from École Polytechnique in France.

He created Xiaopang Notes to organize long-term thinking on artificial intelligence, mathematics, and intelligent systems, and to preserve the questions, derivations, and practical details that deserve more room than a book can always provide.
