# About

I am Gen Tang, a data scientist specializing in AI and big data. I am the author of *Mastering Data Science: From Linear Regression to Deep Learning* and *Deconstructing Large Language Models: From Linear Regression to General Intelligence*, and have served as a technical reviewer for the British technology publisher Packt.

I earned dual bachelor's degrees in Mathematics and Computer Science from Fudan University, followed by dual master's degrees in Economics and Data Science from École Polytechnique in France.
