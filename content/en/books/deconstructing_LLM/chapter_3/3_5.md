## 3.5 Chapter Summary

### 3.5.1 Key Takeaways

Beginning with a simple example, this chapter constructed a linear regression model using both machine-learning and statistical-analysis approaches. During the modeling process, we examined model assumptions, parameter estimation, and result analysis in depth. We also discussed two common modeling errors: overfitting and model hallucinations. Established methods for addressing these errors include cross-validation, hypothesis testing, and the introduction of penalty terms.

In statistics, analyzing a linear regression model usually requires detailed and precise mathematical derivations. Because of space limitations, this book omits the details of those derivations and presents the conclusions directly. Readers interested in the details are encouraged to consult other books, such as *Mathematical Statistics and Data Analysis* by John A. Rice.

As noted at the beginning of this chapter, linear regression is the foundation of all models. The chapter therefore concludes by discussing graphical and mathematical representations of linear models, as well as model persistence. These topics form the foundation for an in-depth discussion of complex models. The chapters that follow will examine, in specific settings, how more complex models can be built from linear models.
