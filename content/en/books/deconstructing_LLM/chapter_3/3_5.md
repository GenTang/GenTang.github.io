## 3.5 Chapter Summary

### 3.5.1 Key Takeaways

Using a simple example, this chapter developed a linear regression model from the perspectives of both machine learning and statistical analysis. Along the way, we examined model assumptions, parameter estimation, and the interpretation of results. We also discussed two common modeling errors: overfitting and model hallucinations. Established methods for addressing these errors include cross-validation, hypothesis testing, and penalty terms.

Statistical analysis of linear regression usually requires detailed and precise mathematical derivations. Because space is limited, this book omits the derivations and presents the conclusions directly. Readers interested in the mathematical details may consult other texts, such as John A. Rice's *Mathematical Statistics and Data Analysis*.

As noted at the beginning of the chapter, linear regression is the foundation of all models. We therefore concluded with graphical and mathematical representations of linear models and an introduction to model persistence. These topics lay the groundwork for a deeper discussion of complex models. The chapters that follow examine how more complex models can be built from linear models in specific settings.
