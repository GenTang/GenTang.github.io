## 13.6 Chapter Summary

### 13.6.1 Key Takeaways

Although the topics in this chapter may appear self-contained, each of the models discussed has inspired or supported neural networks to some degree. Tree models complement neural networks: they can extract features for an overall model and help improve its interpretability. The structure and successful applications of hidden Markov models inspired the use of recurrent neural networks, while the expectation-maximization algorithm helps us understand neural network training more deeply. Although unsupervised learning may seem to have little connection to neural networks, it can be used for preliminary feature processing or for visualizing neural network results. Singular value decomposition not only applies directly to natural language processing but also provides the theoretical foundation for LoRA, a large language model fine-tuning technique. Understanding its mathematical principles makes model fine-tuning techniques easier to comprehend.

Given the limited space, this chapter has covered only a selection of important and illuminating classic models. Many models have not been included, such as the classic support vector machine (SVM). Some model details have also not been discussed, such as penalty terms in decision trees and AdaBoost among ensemble methods. Readers interested in these topics may consult other specialized books, such as *The Elements of Statistical Learning* by Trevor Hastie and his coauthors.
